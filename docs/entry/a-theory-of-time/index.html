A-Theory of Time

Time flows. Physics says: no, it doesn't. One of them is wrong.

2 MIN · EDITED
METAPHYSICS · PAST / NOW / FUTUREOnly now is real. The past is gone; the future isn't yet.Against the block universe, where all times sit side by side.
IN ONE BREATH

The A-theory says tensed facts are fundamental — now moves, the future is open, the past is fixed. The B-theory says only earlier/later relations are real: all moments exist like pages in a book, and flow is an illusion.

The tension

We feel time passing, dread the future, regret the past. Relativity treats time like a fourth dimension with no privileged now. The fight is between lived time and physics time — and neither side will concede.

THE PULL · NEITHER SIDE GIVES

Three positions

PPRESENTISTSONLY NOW EXISTS
GGROWING BLOCKREALITY KEEPS GROWING

P
01
PRESENTISTS

Only the present exists. The past was, the future will be — 'was' and 'will be' do all the work.

G
02
GROWING BLOCK

Past and present exist; the future doesn't yet. Reality grows as the now advances.

E
03
ETERNALISTS

All times are equally real. Passage is perspectival, like 'here' — real to you, not to the world.

THE ARGUMENT, IN THREE LINES
P1We experience genuine passage: events become present, then recede.
P2The best explanation of a universal, vivid experience is that it is veridical.
∴Therefore tensed facts are real. The A-theory is true.

The strongest objection

OBJECTION

Relativity's relativity of simultaneity: there is no single cosmic now. Presentists reply with a privileged foliation, or retreat to a tensed metaphysics without a global present — at a cost.

VS
REPLY

Relativity kills a cosmic now, not a tensed one. Passage survives without a privileged global slice — physics constrains its shape, not its fact.

“Time is what keeps everything from happening at once.”
Ray Cummings, 1921 · quoted by physicists ever since
